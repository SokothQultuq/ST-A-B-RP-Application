WHAT TO LOOK FOR
• Does the overall look and feel land for a Star Trek-inspired game?
• Try the Login page's Discord sign-in and ship selection flow.
• On Engineering and Tactical, try raising/lowering shields, firing a torpedo, or dispatching a repair crew.
• On Medical, try Mass Casualty; on Navigation, try staging a Warp order and hitting Engage.
• On the GD Console, try Yellow/Red Alert and the Casualty Generator, and try building a full ship on the Player Ship Creation screen.
• Would you actually want to run or play a session through something like this?
